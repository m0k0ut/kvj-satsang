# Website Content Brief

## Product goal

Create a trusted public home for Krishnam Vande Jagadgurum. The site should help a Telugu-speaking devotee or family understand the community, see what is taught, meet the teacher, and take one clear next step toward joining a class.

Telegram should remain the live classroom and daily coordination channel. The website should organize the story, programs, schedule, approved resources, and public proof in a form that is easy to discover and share.

## Primary audience

- Telugu-speaking adults seeking guided scripture study and recitation practice
- Family members looking for a structured devotional learning community
- Existing members who need a reliable schedule and approved resources
- Supporters interested in service activities such as annadanam

Do not position the group as women-only unless the organizers confirm that policy. Many visible organizers and participants are women, but the membership rule was not established.

## Recommended positioning

### English

**Guided Telugu scripture learning, practiced together.**

Join a growing devotional community for live classes, recitation practice, revision, parayanam, and seva rooted in the Bhagavatam, Ramayanam, Narayaneeyam, Bhagavad Gita, and related traditions.

### Telugu direction

Use a native Telugu headline rather than a literal machine translation. The approved Telugu copy should express three ideas: guided study, shared practice, and devotional community.

## First-page scope

Start with one strong narrative homepage. Add separate pages only after the core content and assets are approved.

### 1. Hero

- Group name in Telugu and English
- One-sentence purpose
- Primary action: View classes
- Secondary action: Join the Telegram community
- Approved Krishna or group identity image

### 2. What happens here

Use a four-step explanation:

1. Learn through live Telugu classes.
2. Practice with audio, text, and guided recitation.
3. Revise through chapter and verse exercises.
4. Serve and celebrate through parayanam, festivals, and annadanam.

### 3. Programs

Feature the stable learning tracks:

- Srimad Bhagavatam
- Ramayanam and Sundarakanda
- Narayaneeyam and Devi Narayaneeyam
- Bhagavad Gita
- Parayanam and devotional practice

Each program card should state the text, learning format, current level or section, and how to join. Do not publish class times until the organizers confirm a maintainable schedule.

### 4. How classes work

Explain the Telegram learning model in plain language:

- schedule announcement
- live Telegram video class
- shared reading or audio material
- member practice and voice submissions
- revision and questions

This is an important trust section because many newcomers will not know what an online satsang class feels like.

### 5. Teacher and community

Lead with Radha Ma'am's approved biography, teaching approach, and years of study or service. Add the admin and volunteer team only with consent.

Suggested proof line after confirmation:

> Guided by Radha Ma'am and supported by a dedicated volunteer team, the community brings disciplined scripture study into daily life.

### 6. Community in action

Use approved photos from classes, parayanam, festivals, and annadanam. Every photo needs consent and a useful caption. Avoid publishing Telegram screenshots.

### 7. Resources

Offer only group-owned or permission-cleared material. Good first resources could include:

- a newcomer guide
- one sample class recording
- one approved recitation track
- a current program outline
- a public festival or parayanam calendar

### 8. Join

Use one controlled path:

- approved public Telegram link, or
- a short interest form reviewed by an admin

State the primary language, expected class format, time zone, and whether classes are free. These details will reduce unsuitable joins and repeated admin questions.

## Navigation recommendation

For the first version:

- Home
- Programs
- About
- Gallery
- Resources
- Join

On mobile, keep a persistent but unobtrusive `Join the community` action.

## Visual direction

- Deep indigo or midnight blue as the base, drawn from the current logo
- Warm gold for sacred detail and calls to action
- Lotus pink used sparingly
- Soft ivory backgrounds for readable long-form copy
- Telugu and English typefaces chosen for equal legibility
- Real approved photography over generic spiritual stock images
- Krishna, scripture, lamp, lotus, and peacock-feather cues used with restraint

The result should feel devotional and contemporary. Use generous spacing, clear hierarchy, and calm motion. Avoid dense poster layouts, excessive gradients, and animated religious ornaments.

## Content model

Keep copy separate from page code so Telugu and English stay consistent.

Recommended content objects:

- site identity
- teacher profile
- program
- class schedule entry
- resource
- event or seva activity
- testimonial
- gallery item
- join instructions

Each public item should carry an approval status, owner, language, source, and last-reviewed date.

## Initial homepage proof points

Use only after organizer approval:

- 1,600 plus members as of September 2026
- live Telugu classes
- multiple scripture learning tracks
- guided recitation and revision
- online learning connected to seva and devotional gatherings

## Content still needed

- original logo file
- approved Telugu spelling and English transliteration
- teacher biography and portrait
- founder or origin story
- current recurring-program list
- approved weekly schedule and time zone
- approved Telegram join method
- 8 to 12 publication-approved photographs
- 2 to 4 member testimonials with written consent
- ownership confirmation for public PDFs and recordings
- public contact email
- organization or trust details, if applicable

## Recommended first release

Release a bilingual, mobile-first site with the homepage sections above, a simple programs view, an approved gallery, and one join path. Delay member accounts, donations, searchable archives, and automated Telegram integration until the public content and operating process are stable.
